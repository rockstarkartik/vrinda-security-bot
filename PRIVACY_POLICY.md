# Vrinda Security Bot — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 13, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Vrinda Security Bot (“Vrinda”, “we”, “our”, or “the Bot”) is a Discord security and moderation bot designed to help server administrators protect and manage their Discord communities.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Vrinda may process, how that information is used, and what choices users and server administrators have regarding their data.

By using Vrinda, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled in a Discord server, Vrinda may process the following information:

- **Discord identifiers:** Server IDs, user IDs, role IDs, channel IDs, and message IDs.
- **Server configuration:** Security settings, logging channel settings, AutoMod rules, Join Gate settings, and Anti-Nuke configuration.
- **Moderation records:** Actions such as warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans, and related reasons.
- **Security and audit information:** Relevant Discord events, action timestamps, and available audit-log information used to detect potentially unauthorized activity.
- **Message information:** Message content or metadata when necessary for enabled moderation, message logging, or security features.
- **Verification information:** Information needed to operate Join Gate or other verification features, when enabled.

The exact information processed depends on the features configured by server administrators and the permissions and access granted to Vrinda.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

- Detect and respond to unauthorized server actions.
- Prevent spam, raids, malicious links, and other rule violations.
- Apply moderation actions requested or configured by authorized administrators.
- Maintain security logs and moderation records.
- Store server settings and whitelist or authorization configurations.
- Diagnose errors, investigate security incidents, and improve reliability.

We do not claim to collect information that the Bot does not actually access or store.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Vrinda may store configuration, moderation records, security logs, and other feature-related data in its configured database or hosting environment.

We take reasonable steps to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Server administrators should restrict access to security logs and protect their hosting credentials, database files, and bot token.

## 5. Data Sharing

Vrinda does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by Discord as part of the Discord platform, and by the hosting or infrastructure providers used to operate Vrinda. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law or address security and abuse concerns.

We do not promise that data is never shared with infrastructure providers; their handling of data is subject to their own policies and applicable agreements.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the Bot's operation, security, moderation, and configuration purposes, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Server administrators who remove Vrinda from their server or users who have questions about stored information may contact the developer using the contact method listed below.

Deletion requests will be reviewed, and eligible information will be deleted where reasonably possible and legally permitted. Backups or technical records may persist for a limited period where applicable.

## 7. Discord Permissions and Access

Vrinda operates using the Discord permissions and gateway intents enabled for the application and granted by server administrators.

Administrators should grant only the permissions needed for the features they want to use. Some security and moderation features may not function correctly without the necessary permissions or access to relevant Discord events.

Vrinda is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discord Inc.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Vrinda is a community moderation tool, not a service designed to collect personal information directly from children. Users must comply with Discord's applicable age requirements and terms of service.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Vrinda's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at the same URL with a revised “Last Updated” date.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or concerns about Vrinda Security Bot, contact the developer through the official support server or contact method provided by the Bot's developer.
